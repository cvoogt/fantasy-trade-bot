# Brag plan — Fantasy Trade Bot

**What:** A Discord bot for MFL salary-cap dynasty leagues. It scores trades, finds waiver gems, sets lineups, and pings you live when your starters score.
**For:** Dynasty fantasy players in MFL leagues who live in Discord.
**Sets it apart:** Live per-play alerts for your starters (TDs, sacks incl. half sacks, milestones), plus trade verdicts that call out a fleece.
**Funniest claim:** The bot tells you to your face: "You're overpaying."
**Visual hook:** Discord alerts landing in rapid fire on Sunday afternoon.
**Tone:** default. Punchy, sports-broadcast energy, Discord dark UI, sea green (#2E8B57) accent from `EMBED_COLOR`.
**Share caption:** The fantasy bot that tells you when you're getting fleeced.
